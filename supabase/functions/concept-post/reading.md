- synthesis: one sentence that joins the logical and the spatial meaning. It must stand alone.

Rules:
- Natural, precise, professional Hebrew. Not poetic, not translated-sounding. No emoji, no lists inside the parts.

- Spell mathematical terms correctly in Hebrew, for example דלתואיד, היפוציקלואיד, טורוס, רצועת מביוס.

Output: one JSON object and nothing else:
{"logic": "...", "space": "...", "figure_ground": "...", "synthesis": "..."}
